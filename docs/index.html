How Do I Look?

Rate My Look — fun style ratings and tips, all on your device

Welcome. How Do I Look? is a feel-good Android app from BQ Sqwad Technologies. Take or pick a photo, get an entertaining style and presentation score, and receive kind, practical tips — everything stays on your phone.

No accounts. No photo uploads. Just a private way to explore your look and build confidence.

What the app does

  • Rates selfies, outfits, hair, makeup and occasion looks with an overall score and sub-scores
  • Gives specific, body-positive suggestions (lighting, color, framing, pose)
  • Lets you save looks to a virtual wardrobe, compare two photos, and track progress over time
  • Includes a style quiz, color palette ideas, and daily confidence challenges
