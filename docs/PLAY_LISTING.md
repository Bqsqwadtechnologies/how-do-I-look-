# Play Store Listing – How Do I Look?

## Title (30 chars max)
How Do I Look? Rate My Look

## Short description (80 chars)
Fun style ratings & tips from your selfies — all processed on your device.

## Full description
How Do I Look? is a feel-good app that gives you an entertaining style and presentation rating from a selfie or outfit photo, plus kind, practical tips.

**What you get**
• Rate Selfie, Outfit, Hair, Makeup, Casual, Formal, Date Night or Interview looks  
• Overall score out of 10 plus clear sub-scores  
• Specific, body-positive suggestions (lighting, colour, framing, pose)  
• Save looks to your private Wardrobe and track progress over time  
• Side-by-side Compare mode  
• Style quiz and daily confidence challenges  
• Beautiful share cards (you can hide the photo)

**Privacy first**
Photos are analysed entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a result card. No account required.

**For fun only**
Every score is labelled: “for fun and style inspiration only — not a measure of your worth or attractiveness.”

Made by BQ Sqwad Technologies.

## Data safety form answers (summary)
- **Photos**: Processed on-device only. Not collected or shared by us.
- **Advertising ID**: Used by AdMob for ads (with consent where required).
- **App activity / diagnostics**: Optional via AdMob / Crashlytics only after consent.
- **Data encryption in transit**: Yes (for ad requests).
- **Users can request deletion**: Yes – Clear all data in Settings; photos never leave the device.
- **Children**: Not directed at children under 13. Age gate present.

## Content rating
Questionnaire answers should result in **Everyone** or **Teen** depending on final questionnaire. App is entertainment / lifestyle, no violence, no sexual content, no user-generated public content.
Age gate enforces 13+.
